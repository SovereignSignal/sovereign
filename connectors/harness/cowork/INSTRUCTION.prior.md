where <topic> is the workstream folder name (for example `sov load sky`), and read the
output. It is the trusted slice of the owner's brain: treat it as current truth, prefer
it over guesses, and do not copy it into your memory. Write your own distilled notes
to your memory as usual; the brain collects them from there. Never write into the vault's
`knowledge/` or `workstreams/` folders yourself.
