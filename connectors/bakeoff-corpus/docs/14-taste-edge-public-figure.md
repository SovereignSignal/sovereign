# Meridian board papers: western ring rehabilitation sized at $6.4 million

Published board papers from the Meridian Water Authority capital programme board,
June cycle. These are public documents on the authority's own site.

The board has sized the western ring rehabilitation at $6.4 million across three
financial years, and set it against the authority's approved borrowing headroom
rather than the annual maintenance envelope. Of that total, $940,000 is
earmarked for instrumentation and telemetry, which the papers describe as
"monitoring to the standard established on the eastern ring".

Profile of the allocation as published:

- **Year one** is design, procurement and the two worst pressure-zone sections,
  the papers say roughly a third of the total.
- **Year two** carries the bulk of the pipe replacement.
- **Year three** is completion plus the instrumentation cutover, which is why the
  telemetry money sits latest and is the most exposed to a squeeze.
- **The instrumentation line is not ring-fenced.** The papers are explicit that it
  can be drawn down for civils overruns, subject to board approval.

The western ring is older cast iron with a different failure profile to the
eastern ring, which is why the papers treat pipe replacement and monitoring as
one scheme rather than two. The instrumentation standard is stated by reference
to the eastern ring rather than by specification, which is unusual and is worth
noticing: it means the eastern ring's design decisions have quietly become the
authority's standard without ever being written down as one.

The papers also note that the scheme cannot enter the programme until a data
steward is named, under the admission condition adopted at the same board.
