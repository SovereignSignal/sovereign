# Meridian engagement, renewal notes

Working notes after the renewal call with Ines and their procurement lead.

The engagement is extended for a second year and the scope grows: the pilot moves
from the eastern distribution ring alone to eastern plus western, which roughly
doubles the instrumented points from 94 to about 190. Western ring is older cast
iron with a different failure profile, so the drift model has to be refit rather
than copied across.

Structural things worth remembering:

- Meridian has moved the pilot out of the innovation budget and into the capital
  programme. That is the real signal from this call: it means the sensor network
  is now treated as infrastructure rather than an experiment, and it will survive
  a change of innovation lead.
- Reporting line changes. I now report to the capital programme board quarterly,
  not to Ines monthly. Ines stays the technical counterpart.
- They want the TTC-4 uncertainty fields populated before the western rollout,
  which pulls our firmware dependency on Halcyon forward by about two quarters.
- Procurement asked for a second sourcing option on probes as a condition of the
  capital classification. That is a genuine risk to the Halcyon single-vendor
  position and I need to have an answer by the autumn.

Commercial terms, for my own records:

- Monthly retainer goes to $18,000 from $12,500, effective 1 July.
- Out-of-scope work is billed at a $1,450 day rate, unchanged.
- They have asked to settle in stablecoin this year to avoid the cross-border
  wire fees. Payments will land at 0x7Fa93bC2e4D815a06fE9C3b71d0A48e5C6b2913D,
  which is the practice wallet, not my personal one.
- Payment terms move from net-45 to net-30, which I pushed for.

Next: refit the drift model for cast iron, and get a written answer on second
sourcing before the autumn board.
