# Kestrel Water and Vantera Utilities agree all-share merger

Kestrel Water Holdings and Vantera Utilities said on Tuesday they had agreed an
all-share merger that will create the third-largest private water operator in the
Iberian market, serving about 4.1 million connections across Portugal and western
Spain.

Under the terms, Vantera shareholders receive 0.62 Kestrel shares for each Vantera
share, valuing Vantera at roughly 1.9 billion euro. The combined group will be
listed in Lisbon and will keep the Kestrel name. Kestrel's chief executive, Tomas
Alvear, becomes chief executive of the combined group; Vantera's Sofia Brandao
becomes deputy chair.

The boards said the merger would deliver about 74 million euro of annual cost
synergies by the third year, mostly from consolidating the two groups' regional
operating centres from eleven sites to six, and from a single metering and
billing platform.

Analysts were lukewarm. "This is a scale play in a market where scale has not
historically translated into regulated returns," wrote one sector analyst at a
Madrid broker. "The synergy number is credible. The growth story is not."

The transaction is subject to competition clearance in both countries and to
approval by the Portuguese water regulator, which has previously blocked one
consolidation on service-continuity grounds. The companies expect to close in the
first half of 2027.

Neither company operates in North America, and neither is a member of any of the
North American interchange standards bodies.
