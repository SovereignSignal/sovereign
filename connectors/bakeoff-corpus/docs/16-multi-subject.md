# Fortnight notes, 26 June

Three things moved in the last two weeks and they all touch each other.

**Interchange.** The consortium's migration window is now firm: TTC-3 goes to
maintenance status at the autumn 2027 plenary, which is fourteen months out. The
practical deadline is earlier than that, because the conformance lab starts
testing in January 2027 and nobody wants their first lab submission to be their
first TTC-4 build. Vendors are signalling firmware in two quarters. That puts our
own cutover in the first half of 2027 if we want to be boring about it.

**Governance.** The capital programme board's data steward condition lands in the
autumn cycle, and the authority's establishment round runs on a different
calendar to the capital calendar. So the steward for anything we propose in the
autumn is really a post that gets created in next year's staffing plan. Ines
thinks the eastern ring can name a steward retrospectively without difficulty;
the western ring scheme cannot, because there is nobody to name yet.

**The ring.** Twelve month review closed. The number that matters is eleven site
visits against a baseline of ninety-four, and the recommendation to drop the
paired points from a five minute to a fifteen minute interval, which the review
found reproduces every flag we raised. The legacy loop remains the hole in the
coverage and the HX-30 lifecycle notice has now put a date on it.

The three of them compose into one problem rather than three. The interchange
deadline forces a firmware window; the firmware window is the natural moment to
change the sampling interval and to retire the legacy units; and none of that can
be proposed into the capital programme until there is a named steward to propose
it to. So the sequencing is steward first, then firmware window, then interval
and legacy swap together. Getting that order wrong means doing the firmware
window twice.

Nothing here is a decision yet. Next fortnight: get the steward question in front
of Ines properly, and ask Halcyon what their firmware window actually looks like
rather than what the notice says.
