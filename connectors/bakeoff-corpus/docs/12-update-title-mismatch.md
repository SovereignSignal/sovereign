# Halcyon brings the HX-30 support horizon forward to June 2028

Lifecycle notice from Halcyon, posted to their support site and mailed to
registered integrators.

Halcyon has moved the end of support for the HX-30 probe from December 2029 to
June 2028, an eighteen month pull-in. The stated reason is the machining
subcontractor for the HX-30 stem body, which is ending that part line; Halcyon
holds enough stem stock to cover warranty replacements to mid-2028 and no further.

What end of support means in their terms:

- **Firmware.** No further firmware after the 2027.2 release, including no TTC-4
  field support. HX-30 units stay on TTC-3 payloads permanently.
- **Warranty replacement.** Stem-body replacements available until June 2028,
  then not at all. Electronics modules are unaffected and remain serviceable.
- **Calibration service.** Halcyon's own calibration bench drops the HX-30 at the
  same date. Third-party labs are unaffected.
- **No forced removal.** Units keep working. They simply stop being supportable,
  and they cannot carry the TTC-4 uncertainty fields.

The consequence for anyone still running them is that the swap decision moves
from a budgeting question to a dated one. Units on an unpaired loop are the worst
placed, because they are the ones that still depend on an annual bench visit, and
that bench visit is exactly what disappears.

Halcyon is offering a trade-in against HX-40 stock at what they describe as a
material discount, ordered before the end of the calendar year. Priya Raman
confirmed the discount applies to single-unit orders, which matters for anyone
replacing a handful rather than a fleet.

The notice also confirms what was already informally true: the HX-40 becomes the
only supported line, and the four-parameter stem becomes the assumed baseline for
anything Halcyon ships into a distribution network from here.
