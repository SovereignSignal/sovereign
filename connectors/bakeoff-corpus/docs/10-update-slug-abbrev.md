# The Meridian Water Authority capital programme adds a data steward gate

Notes from the papers circulated ahead of the June capital programme board.

The board has adopted a new admission condition, effective for the autumn cycle.
Any instrumented asset entering the capital programme must have a named data
steward on the authority's own staff before capital sign-off. Not a contractor,
not a consultant, and not a role title: a named individual with the post held
open in the establishment.

Why they did it: the assets review found four instrumented schemes across the
authority whose only person who understood the data left, in three cases to
another utility. Two of the four now produce a telemetry feed nobody reads. The
board's language is that an unattended feed is a stranded asset, and stranded
assets are the thing capital governance exists to prevent.

What it changes in practice:

- **Sequencing.** The steward has to be named before sign-off, not after
  handover, which means the authority's staffing decision now gates the
  engineering timeline rather than following it.
- **Consultant scope.** External work can design and commission, but the
  programme will not accept an asset whose operating knowledge lives only with
  the consultant. Knowledge transfer becomes a deliverable with a named
  recipient, not a closing courtesy.
- **Existing programme items.** Anything already admitted has until the spring
  board to name a steward retrospectively, or it drops back to the innovation
  budget, which is the reversal nobody wants.
- **Quarterly reporting.** The steward, not the supplier, presents to the board.

The second-order effect worth remembering is that this makes the authority's
staffing plan a dependency for every proposal, and the staffing plan runs on a
different calendar to the capital calendar. Anything proposed in the autumn is
really being proposed against next year's establishment round.
