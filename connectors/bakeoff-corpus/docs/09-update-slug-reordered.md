# Meridian eastern ring pilot: the twelve-month operating review

Wrote this up after the review meeting so the operating record is in one place.

Twelve months of continuous operation on the eastern distribution ring closed at
the end of May. The headline is that the deployment is no longer behaving like a
pilot, and the numbers are the argument.

- **Coverage.** 94 measurement points at commissioning; 91 of the 94 have run a
  complete drift-paired record for the full twelve months. The three gaps are all
  the same chamber, which floods, and all three are inspection failures rather
  than instrument failures.
- **Truck rolls.** Eleven site visits in twelve months against a pre-pilot
  baseline of one visit per point per year. That is the drift-pair claim landing
  in the operating budget rather than in a slide.
- **Differential breaks.** Nine differential-break flags. Seven were real: five
  fouled stems and two cable faults. Two were the fit chasing a genuine step
  change in the water after a main was recharged, which is the known failure mode
  of the ninety-day window and the reason the window is not shorter.
- **Interval.** The 5 minute interval is more than the ring needs. Fifteen
  minutes reproduces every flag we raised and cuts the payload volume by two
  thirds. Recommendation to the authority is to move to 15 minutes on the paired
  points at the next firmware window.
- **Legacy loop.** Still unpaired, still on an annual lab visit, still the only
  part of the ring the method does not cover.

Operationally the change from here is that reporting moves from a monthly
technical summary to a quarterly one, and the weekly digest stays as it is. The
deployment gets treated as standing infrastructure rather than a trial, and the
review recommends the same instrument design be used unchanged when the western
ring is instrumented.

One open item: nothing in the record answers what happens to the differential fit
when a pair straddles two pressure zones, because the eastern ring is a single
zone. The western ring is not, so that has to be settled before the design is
copied across.
