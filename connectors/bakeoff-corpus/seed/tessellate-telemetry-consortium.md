# Tessellate Telemetry Consortium

**Why this matters:** The owner holds a seat on its calibration working group, so its spec revisions set the ground rules for every Meridian deployment.

Standards body that maintains the municipal sensor interchange spec used across North American water utilities. Founded 2017; 63 member utilities and 14 vendor members.

- **Working groups:** calibration (the owner's seat), transport, conformance, procurement language.
- **Spec line:** TTC-3 shipped 2024-11 and is the version currently deployed at Meridian.
- **Cadence:** plenary twice a year, spring and autumn; revisions ship after the autumn plenary.
- **Chair:** Marta Reyes-Hale, calibration working group, since 2023.
- **Conformance:** self-attested; no independent test lab as of the TTC-3 line.

Living detail sits in `workstreams/meridian-pilot/`.
