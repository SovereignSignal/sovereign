# Meridian Water Authority capital programme

**Why this matters:** It is the funding and governance track the owner's work now sits inside, so its rules decide what the practice can propose and how long an engagement survives.

The authority's multi-year infrastructure spending track, distinct from the innovation budget that funds one-off trials. Governed by a capital programme board that meets quarterly.

- **Board:** quarterly, chaired by the authority's director of assets.
- **Admission test:** an asset enters the programme only once it is treated as infrastructure rather than a trial.
- **Consequence:** programme items survive a change of innovation lead; trials do not.
- **Reporting:** quarterly to the board, not monthly to a technical counterpart.
