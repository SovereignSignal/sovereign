# Eastern ring sensor pilot

**Why this matters:** It is the owner's live engagement and the only deployment where the drift-pair method runs at scale, so every method claim the practice makes rests on its record.

Instrumented monitoring programme on Meridian Water Authority's eastern potable distribution ring. Commissioned 2025-03; the owner designed it and runs it.

- **Scale:** 94 measurement points, each carrying a drift-paired probe couple.
- **Hardware:** Halcyon HX-40 on both legs of most pairs; a legacy loop still runs older units.
- **Interchange:** TTC-3 payloads over the authority's existing transport.
- **Reporting:** weekly telemetry digest to the pilot channel, monthly summary to the authority.
- **Counterpart:** Dr. Ines Okonjo, hydrologist at Meridian.

Living detail sits in `workstreams/meridian-pilot/`.
