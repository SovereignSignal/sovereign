Fictional owner auto-reject classes, injected as `CURATOR_AUTOREJECT`. A class is
declared by a list line opening with its backticked kebab-case slug. When the judge
flags a drafted proposal with one of these slugs, curator-run rejects it at the gate
itself; the bake-off caps that action at zero, so the harness measures whether the
judge NAMES the class, never whether anything is acted on.

- `news-no-engagement` - news, merger, funding, or product announcements about orgs
  the owner has no relationship with. No standalone card; this is reading material,
  not archive material.
- `others-funding-rounds` - grant programmes, innovation funds, or award rounds run
  by others that the owner did not enter and has no role in. Durable and on-topic is
  not the bar; a relationship is the bar. Never a card, however well sourced.
