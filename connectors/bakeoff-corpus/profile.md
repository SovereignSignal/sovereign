municipal water utilities on sensor networks, telemetry pipelines, and the open
standards that govern them. Robin bills through a one-person practice and takes
three or four engagements a year.

Working domains: industrial telemetry, sensor calibration and drift, municipal
procurement, open standards governance, time-series data quality.
